SMS Opt-In

Information about transactional text messages from APPtivity Labs products.

Last updated: July 24, 2026

How users opt in

Users opt in by entering their mobile number in an APPtivity Labs product and requesting a text message for account verification, security, or another service-related purpose. Consent applies only to the requested transactional messages.

Message types and frequency

Messages may include one-time passcodes, account-verification notices, security alerts, and service-related updates. Message frequency varies based on account activity and requested services.

Rates, consent, and opt-out

Message and data rates may apply. Consent to receive SMS messages is not a condition of purchase. Reply STOP to opt out. Reply HELP for assistance.

Mobile information privacy

Mobile numbers, SMS opt-in data, and consent records will not be sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

We may share mobile information with service providers only as needed to deliver requested messages, maintain consent records, process opt-out requests, protect our services, and support legal compliance.

Contact

For SMS assistance, reply HELP or email hi@goapptivity.com. See our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service for more information.
