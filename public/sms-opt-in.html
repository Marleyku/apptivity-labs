SMS Opt-In Disclosure

This page explains how APPtivity Labs, LLC uses SMS for transactional account, security, and service-related purposes.

Last updated: July 23, 2026

Program description

APPtivity Labs, LLC may offer SMS messaging for its administrative website and software products, including APPtivity, FavorBank, and Miles2Go. SMS messages are intended for transactional account-verification, security, and service-related communications.

Types of messages

You may receive messages related to account verification, login or security alerts, product setup, service notifications, reminders you request, or other operational communications needed to provide the service.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies based on your account activity, product settings, requested reminders, and service needs.

Costs

Message and data rates may apply. Contact your mobile carrier for details about your messaging plan.

Opt-out instructions

You can opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying STOP. After you opt out, you may receive a confirmation message.

Help instructions

Reply HELP for assistance, or contact APPtivity Labs at hi@goapptivity.com.

Consent

Consent to receive SMS messages is not a condition of purchase. If you do not consent to SMS, you may still contact us by email and use available non-SMS account or support options where offered.

Privacy

Mobile numbers, SMS opt-in data, and consent records will not be sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

For more details, review our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
