SMS Opt-Out

How to stop transactional text messages from APPtivity Labs products.

Last updated: August 15, 2026

How to opt out

Reply STOP to any APPtivity Labs text message to opt out. You may receive one final confirmation message, and then no further messages will be sent unless you opt in again.

Get help

Reply HELP for assistance or email hi@goapptivity.com. Message and data rates may apply.

Opt in again

If you later want to receive requested transactional messages again, follow the opt-in process in the relevant APPtivity Labs product. See our SMS Opt-In page for details.

Privacy and terms

See our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service for more information about how we handle mobile information and provide our services.
