To use {{PRODUCT_NAME}}, you must consent to privacy-preserving product analytics, session diagnostics or replay where enabled, and error monitoring. ## No sale, trade, or exchange

To the fullest extent permitted by law, {{BRAND_LEGAL}} does not sell, trade, or exchange personal information for money or other valuable consideration, and does not share personal information with third parties for their independent marketing. We retain account and operational data as needed to provide the Service, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. Analytics and error events are retained according to our processors’ configurations and our operational needs; we do not keep them longer than reasonably necessary for those purposes.
